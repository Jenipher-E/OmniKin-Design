OmniKin
Style Guide
Colour system · Typography · Application rules
The single source of truth for how OmniKin looks and reads — across the product UI, marketing, and documents. One palette, one type system, applied consistently so that “privacy-first, all-in-one, trustworthy” is felt before it is read.
How to use this guide.  Every visual decision maps to a named token (e.g. Teal 700) with a fixed hex value. Always reference the token, never a one-off colour. The anchor of the whole system is the brand teal #1C6E78, used for the title and primary brand expression throughout.
[Heading1] 1.  Brand essence
OmniKin is a calm, trustworthy household operating system. The visual language has three jobs: feel safe (privacy is the brand), feel unified (one app, not a patchwork), and feel inclusive (as at home in Lagos or Nairobi as in Berlin or Paris).
[ListParagraph] Calm & trustworthy:  deep teals, generous white space, no visual clutter, no ad-style noise.
[ListParagraph] Unified:  one consistent palette and type scale across all fourteen features and every surface.
[ListParagraph] Inclusive & light:  high-contrast, legible type and lightweight assets that work on low-end devices and small screens.
[Heading1] 2.  Major brand colours

1.  Teal 700 — #1C6E78  (primary, dominant).  The dominant brand colour and the foundation of the identity — logo, primary surfaces and the document/screen title. Its full tonal range (Teal 900–100, listed in section 3.1) extends this one colour across the whole product.
2.  Coral — #E2683C  (secondary, used sparingly).  The warm secondary that brings energy and human warmth. Reserved for the primary call-to-action and key highlights — deliberately rare so it always signals “act here”.
3.  Cornflower Blue — #2E5A87  (secondary, sub-headers).  The cool secondary (Google Docs: “Dark cornflower blue 3”). Its defined role is sub-headers — H2 sub-headings throughout the product and documents — giving a clear teal-to-blue step down the heading hierarchy.

[Heading1] 5.  Typography
One typeface family does everything. Clear hierarchy comes from size and weight, not from switching fonts.
[Heading2] 5.1  Typefaces
[ListParagraph] Product UI (primary):  Inter. Chosen for excellent small-size legibility, a low-bandwidth variable-font build, broad language coverage (including the Latin set behind Swahili and other localisations) and tabular figures for expense tracking. Stack: Inter, “Segoe UI”, Roboto, system-ui, Arial, sans-serif.
[ListParagraph] Documents (Word / Google Docs):  Arial as the rendering-safe equivalent of Inter, so reports and specs match the product without font-substitution surprises. Calibri is an acceptable fallback.
[ListParagraph] Monospace (codes & data):  Roboto Mono / system monospace for OTPs, IDs, hex values and anything that must align by character.
Default character style: sentence case for everything except the wordmark; left-aligned; never justified. Default body line-height is 1.5; headings 1.2–1.3. Em dashes ( — ) and true smart quotes ( “ ” ‘ ’ ) throughout.

[Heading1] 6.  Default text & component styles
The baseline rules every screen and document inherits unless a role above overrides them.
[ListParagraph] Default paragraph:  Arial / Inter Regular, 11 pt (16 px), Ink #1E2A2D, line-height 1.5, left-aligned, 8–10 pt space after. No first-line indents.
[ListParagraph] Emphasis:  bold uses Ink or Teal 800 for lead-in labels; italics for captions and figure references only. Never underline for emphasis — underline means link.
[ListParagraph] Links:  Teal 500 #2A8C99, underline on hover/press; visited links keep the same colour (no purple).
[ListParagraph] Lists:  teal bullet glyph, 0.25” hanging indent; numbered lists use the same indent with a period.
[ListParagraph] Inputs:  white fill, Hairline #DCE5E6 1px border, Teal 500 focus ring, Error #C9453B for invalid state with a message in the same colour.
[ListParagraph] Cards & panels:  white surface on Canvas background, Hairline border, 8–12 px radius, soft shadow; section/info panels use Teal 100 fill.
[ListParagraph] Tables:  Teal 700 header with white text (or Teal 100 for a lighter look), Hairline cell borders, Canvas zebra striping, 8/14 px cell padding.
[Heading1] 7.  Accessibility & guardrails
[ListParagraph] Maintain WCAG AA contrast: Teal 700, Teal 800, Cornflower Blue, Ink, Coral, Green and Error all clear AA for normal text on white (Cornflower Blue ≈ 7:1). Teal 300, Teal 100 and Muted are for large text, borders or fills only — never body text.
[ListParagraph] Never rely on colour alone to carry meaning — pair functional colours with an icon or label (important for accessibility and for printed / low-colour devices).
[ListParagraph] Keep Coral rare: it loses its “act now” power if it appears more than once or twice per screen.
[ListParagraph] No ad-style visual noise, no gradients on text, no more than two decorative accent colours in one view (Cornflower Blue is structural for sub-heads, not a decorative accent) — restraint is the brand.
[ListParagraph] Assets stay lightweight (SVG icons, variable fonts, compressed images) so the system performs on low-end devices, in line with the African-market strategy.
One palette. One type system. Applied the same way every time — that consistency is what makes OmniKin read as trustworthy.
