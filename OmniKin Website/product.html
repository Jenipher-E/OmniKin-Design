The product

A household operating system, not another app to check.

OmniKin replaces the group chat, the fridge list, the shared calendar nobody updates and the spreadsheet only one person understands. Same members, same permissions, same privacy rules, everywhere in the app.

The problem

A household runs on information that lives in the wrong places.

It sits in one person's head, in a chat thread that scrolls away, on a list stuck to a fridge in a different building from the shop. So things get bought twice, chores get missed, and one person ends up as the household's single point of failure.

Five apps that do not talk

A list app, a calendar, a chore chart and a money tracker. Nothing connects, so nothing is ever fully up to date.

One shared password

Most family apps put the whole household behind a single login, which makes per-person roles, child safety and any real audit trail impossible.

Built for perfect conditions

A new phone and fast data are assumed. In a basement supermarket or on intermittent mobile data, the app is a spinner.

Paid for with attention

Free family apps are funded by ads and profiling, using data about children, health, money and location.

The value

What a household actually gets.

Four outcomes we hold ourselves to. If a feature does not serve one of them, it does not ship.

Less to hold in your head

The household remembers instead of one person remembering for everyone.

A fairer share of the work

The load becomes visible, so it can be discussed and moved without a fight.

Works where you are

Opens offline with cached data, syncs quietly later. No signal, no problem.

Safe for everyone in it

Individual logins, protective defaults for children, visibility set per record.

In use

A week in a household, four screens.

Tap through the previews. This is the real interface language: white surfaces, teal for structure, coral reserved for the one thing that needs a decision.

★ Differentiator

Notice what the app never does. It does not rank people, award points, or nag. A heads-up is a heads-up, and every suggestion waits for a human to confirm it.

Privacy

Privacy is the product, not a policy page.

OmniKin holds some of the most sensitive information a family has: where the children are, what they eat, what the household earns and spends. That is exactly why the business model has no room for advertising.

Ask us anything about data

No ads, no profiling, no data sales

Revenue comes from households choosing to subscribe, and from nothing else.

Individual identity for every member

There is no shared household password anywhere in the product, so every action is attributable and every child is protected by design.

Visibility set per record

Whole household, chosen members, or you only. Set on the thing itself, not buried in a settings screen.

Built to the stricter standard

GDPR, including children's protections, applied across every market we operate in rather than only where it is required.
