Mission and vision

Running a home is real work. Software should carry some of it.

Most families hold their household together with a group chat, a paper list and someone's memory. The tools that promise to help either sell attention or assume everyone has a new phone and fast data. We started OmniKin because neither is good enough.

Our mission

To give every household one private place to organise life together, on whatever phone they own and whatever the connection.

Our vision

Homes where the mental load is shared rather than carried by one person, and where family data stays with the family.

What makes it different

Three things the other family apps do not do.

The fair-load view

OmniKin can show how household work is actually spread across the people in it, and suggest a rebalance. It is a conversation starter, not a scoreboard, and nothing moves unless someone agrees to it.

Out-of-stock intelligence

When an item is not on the shelf, the list does not just sit there. It offers a sensible swap, parks the item, and tells whoever asked for it, so the trip is not wasted.

Privacy you can point at

Every member has their own login, including children. Visibility is set per record: whole household, chosen members, or you only. There is no shared family password anywhere in the product.

★ Differentiator

Offline is a first-class state, not an error screen. The app opens to clearly-labelled cached data with no network round-trip, and queued changes sync when the connection returns. That single decision is what makes OmniKin usable on a crowded commute, in a basement supermarket, or on intermittent data.
