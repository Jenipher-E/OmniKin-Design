Upcoming modules

What we are building next, and in what order.

OmniKin ships as a connected set of modules rather than one big release. Foundations first, then the modules that depend on them. This page stays current, so you can see exactly where the product is.

Now

Foundations

Accounts, household roles, lists, tasks, and budgets and expenses. The layer everything else inherits.

Next

The shared week

Notifications, timetable, messaging, and the meal and family recipe planner.

Then

Time, documents and trust

Calendar, document organiser, location sharing, the audit trail and the privacy dashboard.

In build

Budgets and expenses

Household budgets by period and category, with expenses logged offline and a private overspend heads-up based only on your own household's history. Mobile-money-aware from the start, without OmniKin ever moving money itself.

Next

Notifications

One calm daily digest instead of a stream of alerts, with quiet hours and per-channel preferences for every member.

Next

Timetable

School and activity timetables as first-class recurring structure, so a term of commitments is entered once and then simply exists.

Next

Messaging

Household conversation in the same place as the work it is about, scoped to each member’s role rather than one open group chat.

Next

Meal and family recipe

Plan the week’s meals, keep dietary needs and allergies flagged, and hold the household’s own recipes. Missing ingredients go straight to the shopping list, and optional cost estimates can feed the groceries budget.

Then

Shared calendar

One family calendar where visibility is set per event. A therapy appointment can stay private while the school run is visible to everyone, with recurring events and invitations that respect each member’s role.

Then

Document organiser

A place for the things a household has to keep: permits, insurance, prescriptions, school forms and expiry dates, with per-member visibility.

Then

Location sharing, Find my Family

Opt-in place sharing between members, so a parent can see that a child arrived safely. Always visible to the person being shared, never a silent feed.

Then

Audit trail and privacy dashboard

A readable record of who created, edited or deleted what, and a plain-language view of what OmniKin holds, who can see it, and how to export or delete it.

How we decide the order

Two things set the sequence. First, dependencies: nothing can exist before accounts, roles and permissions. Second, what households tell us hurts most right now.

We publish the order rather than teasing it, because a household deciding whether to move its life into OmniKin deserves to know what is actually there today.

★ Every module, same rules

A new module never brings its own login, its own permissions or its own privacy settings. It inherits the household's, which is why adding one does not add admin work.
